Tandem Perovskite-Silicon Photovoltaics Cross 34.8% Efficiency Threshold

Published by Senior Energy Analyst • Updated September 2026 • Peer Reviewed

Commercial silicon solar modules have historically operated under the single-junction Shockley-Queisser theoretical ceiling of 33.7%. Recent certified testing from the National Renewable Energy Laboratory confirms that dual-junction tandem stacks combining wide-bandgap perovskite top cells with localized passivated silicon bottom cells have achieved conversion efficiencies above 34.85%.
